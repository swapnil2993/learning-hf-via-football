
# 🧠 Zero-Shot Classification

## Core Idea
- Better wording = better results  
- AI systems are **guided, not just trained**

---

## Traditional ML vs Zero-Shot

### Traditional ML
- Collect data  
- Label data  
- Train model  

### Zero-Shot
- Define labels  
- Run inference  

---

## Key Insight
- Extract **structure from unstructured data**
- Turn text → meaningful categories

---

## AI System Thinking

> AI systems are **pipelines, not models**

### Typical Pipeline
Text → Chunking → Classification → Embeddings → Retrieval  

---

## What Zero-Shot Teaches

- Models are just **components**
- **System design > model choice**
- Language = control mechanism for AI
- Fast experimentation without retraining
