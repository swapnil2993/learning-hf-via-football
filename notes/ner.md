# 🧠 Named Entity Recognition (NER) — Notes

## 📌 What is NER?

Named Entity Recognition (NER) is an NLP task that identifies and classifies key elements in text into predefined categories such as:

- Person (e.g., Johan Cruyff)
- Organization (e.g., Ajax)
- Location (e.g., Italy)

---

## ⚙️ What NER Does

- Scans unstructured text
- Detects important entities (who, where, what)
- Assigns labels (PER, ORG, LOC)
- Outputs structured information from raw text

---

## 🔄 Example

### Input:

"Johan Cruyff revolutionized football at Ajax"

### Output:

- Johan Cruyff → Person
- Ajax → Organization

---

## 🧩 Role in AI Pipelines

Text → NER → Clean → Structure → Store

NER acts as the **first step** in converting raw text into usable data.

---

## 🚀 How NER Helps in AI Journey

### 1. From Text → Meaning

- Moves beyond plain text
- Identifies key actors and entities

---

### 2. Enables Structured Thinking

Instead of:

- Unstructured paragraphs ❌

You get:

- Entities and relationships ✅

---

### 3. Foundation for Advanced Systems

NER powers:

- Knowledge graphs
- Search engines
- Recommendation systems
- RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)

---

### 4. Teaches Model Limitations

- Models may miss or misclassify entities
- Requires post-processing (cleaning, deduplication)

---

### 5. Builds Real Engineering Skills

- Data extraction
- Pipeline design
- Output validation

---

## 🧠 Key Insight

> NER is not the final output — it is the **bridge from raw text to structured knowledge**
